Portals

For the Konsool

annejan.com

A Portal-like puzzle game for the Konsool. Twenty test chambers, two portals, and GLaDOS watching. Then stories: packs of new chambers told from a desk between the rounds.

Ghost races against your best runs, and other people's. Lifts between the chambers. Cubes, buttons, turrets, faith plates, light bridges and energy pellets. A chamber editor on the badge itself. GLaDOS speaks, through SAM, the C64's Software Automatic Mouth.

Put it on your Konsool

  1. Connect the Konsool to this computer with USB.
  2. On the Konsool, press the purple diamond: a USB icon shows top right. That is BadgeLink mode.
  3. Press Install and pick Tanmatsu in the list: that is your Konsool.

    Straight from the Konsool's own app repository, as its app store would: Portals, and Graceloader, the loader it runs under, if your Konsool does not have that version yet. It works in Chrome and Edge. On Linux the browser may only open the Konsool with badgelink's udev rule, 60-badgelink.rules, in /etc/udev/rules.d/.

    Stories

    After the twenty test chambers: stories. Each is a pack of new chambers with its own music, and some are told from a desk between the rounds. They go on the SD card; put them on your Konsool from scene.rs/stories, the same way as Portals itself.

    Human in the loop

    Aperture's test generator needs a human to break its drafts. You are the human. From a DOS desk: GLaDOS's flawed chambers to cheese, a debugger for the broken ones, mail, and a birthday cake that keeps moving.

    The tests are over. The testing is not. Chambers for after the twenty: a parcel to post, pellets to relay in the dark, turrets on the night shift, then a production floor, a canyon and a spire.
