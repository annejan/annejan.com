Your Konsool

Every app in the Konsool's app repository, the one its launcher's app store uses. Install, update or remove them from here, over USB. New to the Konsool? Badge.Team's Konsool docs. No Konsool yet? It is made and sold by Nicolai Electronics, as the Tanmatsu.

  1. Connect the Konsool to this computer with USB.
  2. On the Konsool, press the purple diamond: a USB icon shows top right. That is BadgeLink mode.
  3. Press Connect and pick Tanmatsu in the list (that is your Konsool), to see what it has. Or press Install on any app.

It works in Chrome and Edge. On Linux the browser may only open the Konsool with badgelink's udev rule, 60-badgelink.rules, in /etc/udev/rules.d/.
